How donation works

Eyes are donated after death, never during life. Here is what happens.

  1. Pledge

    Register here and tell your family. Their consent is what makes it happen.

  2. Call the eye bank

    When the time comes, your family phones the nearest eye bank as soon as possible.

  3. Gift of sight

    Trained staff remove the corneas in about 20 minutes. The face is not disfigured.

    Common questions

    Short answers to what families ask most.

    Can I donate if I wear glasses or had cataract surgery?

    Yes. Most people can donate, including those with poor eyesight, glasses or past cataract surgery. A doctor makes the final check.

    Does eye donation delay the funeral?

    No. Removal takes about 20 minutes and can be done at home or in a hospital.

    Can someone donate while alive?

    No. Eye donation is only possible after death. Pledging now records your wish so your family can act quickly.

    Why do you ask for blood group?

    It helps the campaign keep a complete donor record. Cornea matching does not depend on blood group.
